    06 — Product & Tabletop

    Tabletop & product

    Glass doesn’t “take” light, it reflects and refracts it. So we don’t light the bottle directly; we light the background or panels beside it so the edges of the glass show up as lines. Matte objects, on the other hand, need light falling on them directly.
